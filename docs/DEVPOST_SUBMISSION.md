## Working title

LifeBridge — bilingual family-care coordination with dignity, consent, and clear
accountability.

## Problem

Care responsibilities are often distributed across family members,
organizations, and volunteers. Fragmented schedules and handoffs can make
ownership, timing, consent, and emergency context difficult to understand.

## Planned solution

LifeBridge aims to provide:

- a shared household dashboard;
- assignable care tasks and timeline handoffs;
- reminders and notification acknowledgements;
- consent-aware access and audit history;
- emergency and community-support workflows;
- accessible Vietnamese and English experiences.

These are planned capabilities. The final submission must identify exactly which
are implemented and demonstrable.

## Technical direction

- Windows-first Codex App development workflow.
- TypeScript monorepo with independently deployable services.
- API gateway plus service-owned data and versioned events.
- Google Stitch used only as reviewed design input.
- synthetic bilingual fixtures and traceable public research sources.
