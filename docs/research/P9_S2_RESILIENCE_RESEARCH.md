## Decisions and non-goals

No Background Sync, service-worker mutation replay, persistent generic queue,
new cache/database, auto retry, legal interpretation or guaranteed network
detection is introduced. Tests must cover false online hints, lost response,
double submit, reconnect races, revocation and authoritative reconciliation.
